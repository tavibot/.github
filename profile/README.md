# Tavi

**The all-in-one bot for better Discord communities.**

Tavi helps Discord communities stay organized, welcoming, and active from one place. Whether you are building a new server or improving an established one, Tavi brings practical tools and thoughtful automation together so your team can spend less time managing the server and more time growing the community.

## What Tavi helps with

- **Community management** — keep your server organized and easier to run.
- **Moderation** — support a safer, more welcoming space for members.
- **Automation** — reduce repetitive work with dependable workflows.
- **Utility** — give members useful tools without filling your server with separate bots.
- **Customization** — shape Tavi around the way your community works.

## Get started

Visit [tavibot.app](https://tavibot.app) to learn more about Tavi and bring it to your Discord community.

## Built for communities

Tavi is designed for community owners, moderators, and members alike: straightforward to use, flexible enough to fit different servers, and focused on making everyday Discord management better.
